# Revisions

Where a report goes when there is no channel to the skill: its repository unreachable, no permission to file, or an offline session. The substance is the same either way, and `SKILL.md` says what it is under Revise.

Append-only. Close a report by appending a line that names the outcome, never by editing the block: the history of a disagreement explains the rule that came out of it.
